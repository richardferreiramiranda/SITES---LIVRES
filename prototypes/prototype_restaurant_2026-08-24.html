Our Story

Twelve years of one open kitchen

Amber & Oak started as a single wood-fired oven and a short list of things done well. That hasn't changed — we still cook everything in view of the room, plate by plate.

  • Ingredients sourced from farms within 80 miles
  • Menu rewritten weekly with the season
  • Private dining room for groups up to 20

Ready for a table?

We hold a portion of seating for walk-ins nightly, but reservations are recommended Thursday through Saturday.
