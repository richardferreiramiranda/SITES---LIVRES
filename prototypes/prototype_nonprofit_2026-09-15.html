What we do

Three programs, one mission

Every program is designed with the community it serves, and run by a mix of staff and trained local volunteers.

🥗

Food Security

Weekly pantry distributions and hot-meal programs for families facing food insecurity.

  • 6 neighborhood pantry sites
  • Fresh produce partnerships
  • No paperwork required
🌱

Youth Mentorship

After-school mentoring, tutoring, and enrichment for kids ages 8–17.

  • 1:4 mentor-to-student ratio
  • Homework help & tutoring
  • Summer enrichment camp
🧰

Job Readiness

Resume workshops, interview coaching, and a paid apprenticeship pipeline for adults.

  • Free workshop series
  • Employer partner network
  • Paid apprenticeship track
